Privacy Policy – Viscerift Auto Poster

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Who uses this app

Viscerift Auto Poster is used only by the owner of the Viscerift YouTube channel to upload and schedule the channel's own videos. It is not available to the public.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload videos, titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails to the owner's channel (scope youtube.upload);
  • read the owner's channel name and the status of videos it uploaded (scope youtube.readonly).

The app does not access, collect or process data about any other YouTube user or viewer.

How data is stored

The OAuth token and a list of uploaded video IDs are stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sent to any server other than Google/YouTube, and no data is sold or shared with third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google and YouTube policies

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
